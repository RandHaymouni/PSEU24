**Activities and Attractions:**
I’m excited to share my plan for exploring Barcelona during my 5-day trip. Here’s how I’m planning to divide my time to make the most of this beautiful city:

**Day 1:** Arrival and Initial Exploration
On the first day, I’ll arrive in Barcelona and check into my accommodation at TOC Hostel Barcelona. 
After settling in, I’ll start with a leisurely stroll around La Rambla, one of the most famous streets in the city. 
It’s full of life, with street performers, vendors, and cafes. From there, I’ll head to Plaça de Catalunya, the central square, 
which is a great place to get my bearings. I’ll end the evening exploring the El Born district, which is known for its lively atmosphere, 
charming streets, and boutique shops.

**Day 2:** Gaudí’s Masterpieces
The second day will be all about Gaudí, one of Barcelona’s most famous architects.
I’ll begin my day with a visit to Sagrada Família, the stunning basilica that’s been under construction for over a century. 
I’m really looking forward to experiencing its incredible design up close. Afterward, I’ll head to Park Güell, 
where I can wander through the beautiful gardens and enjoy the colorful mosaics. In the afternoon, I plan to visit Casa Batlló and Casa Milà (La Pedrera), 
two of Gaudí’s most famous buildings, each with their own unique architectural style. I’ll finish my day by taking a walk along Passeig de Gràcia, 
where I can enjoy the elegance of Barcelona's shops and architecture.

**Day 3:** Gothic Quarter and Montjuïc
On my third day, I’ll explore the Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic), 
one of the oldest parts of the city. I’m excited to walk through its narrow, 
winding streets and visit the Barcelona Cathedral and Plaça del Rei. 
I’ll also take the opportunity to explore the Picasso Museum or the Museu d’Historia de Barcelona to learn more about the city's rich history.
In the afternoon, I’ll head to Montjuïc to visit Montjuïc Castle and the Olympic Stadium. 
I also plan to check out Poble Espanyol, an open-air museum that showcases different Spanish architectural styles. In the evening, 
I’ll watch the Magic Fountain of Montjuïc light and music show, which I hear is truly spectacular.

**Day 4:** Day Trip to Montserrat or Girona
On the fourth day, I’m planning to take a day trip to Montserrat, 
a mountain range about an hour outside of Barcelona. 
I’m particularly excited to visit the Montserrat Monastery and take a cable car to the top for stunning views of the surrounding landscape. 
Alternatively, I might choose to visit Girona, a beautiful medieval city with picturesque streets. 
I can’t wait to visit the Arab Baths, walk along the city walls, and explore the Girona Cathedral.

**Day 5:** Coastal Relaxation and More Sights
On the final day, I’m planning to head to Costa Brava for a day of relaxation along the coast. 
I’ll explore the charming seaside villages, such as Tossa de Mar or Lloret de Mar, and enjoy some time on the beach. 
If I decide to stay in Barcelona, I’ll visit Park de la Ciutadella, where I can relax by the lake and visit the Barcelona Zoo. 
I also plan to explore the Museu Nacional d'Art de Catalunya (MNAC), housed in the impressive Palau Nacional.
To end the day, I’ll have dinner at one of the halal restaurants like Zeeshan Kebabish or Bombay Spicy to enjoy some delicious local food.
